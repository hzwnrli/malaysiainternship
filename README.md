# LPG Dealer Wars

A browser game where you run a Premier LPG Dealer and compete with three rival dealers for customers over 30 days.

**Play:** open `lpg-dealer-wars/index.html` in any browser. It's a single file and needs no install.

## Customers and products
| Customer | Products |
|---|---|
| 🏠 Household | C14 (subsidised) |
| 🍜 Restaurant | C14C, C50 |
| ☕ Lobby / Café | C14C |
| 🏨 Hotel | C50, Bulk LPG |
| 🏬 Hospitality Mall | Bulk LPG, C50 |
| 🏭 Manufacturing | Bulk LPG, F14, C50 |

## How it works
- Pitch customers with a standard offer or a discount deal. Each pitch uses one sales visit.
- End the day to make deliveries. Truck capacity limits how many orders you can fill, and missed orders make customers unhappy.
- Buy a bulk road tanker to supply BULK and the F14 programme to supply forklift cylinders.
- Rival dealers try to take your unhappy customers. Customer-care visits keep them loyal.
- **Compliance:** subsidised C14 is for households only. You can offer it to commercial customers as a shortcut, but that risks enforcement raids, fines and lost reputation.

Prices and numbers in the game are simplified for play and are not real prices.
